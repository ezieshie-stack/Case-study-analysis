# Submission checklist

Run through this on 30 or 31 October. Tick every line before uploading.

## Contents
- [ ] Short communication plan, with course concepts cited
- [ ] At least four texts, each for a different target group: the feature plus the Instagram, Facebook and LinkedIn posts
- [ ] The three posts share one core message and one photograph, each adapted in length, tone, crop and call to action
- [ ] The plan explains why each platform suits its audience, with the Swedish usage figures
- [ ] One of them is the feature based on an interview
- [ ] Each text is labelled with audience, format and intended platform (the three lines at the top of each file)
- [ ] Reference list, APA 7th, editions actually used
- [ ] Gaps in research mentioned briefly in the plan

## Accuracy
- [ ] Every quotation matches the verification log in `fieldwork/interview_notes.md`
- [ ] Every fact traces to `fieldwork/source_log.md` or a timed observation note
- [ ] Names, roles and the place's name are spelled as the people and organisation spell them
- [ ] Dates, prices, hours and addresses checked against the official site this week
- [ ] Interviewee's attribution preference respected (full name / first name / none)

## Honesty and balance
- [ ] The feature includes a perspective other than the main interviewee's
- [ ] At least one "what could be better" finding appears in the feature and the plan

## Language
- [ ] Each text read aloud once; stumbles rewritten
- [ ] Each text opens differently and in a way that suits its reader
- [ ] No `[[...]]` placeholders remain (run `python3 place-branding/build_portfolio.py --check`)
- [ ] No `> ✎` guidance lines remain in the assembled document

## Formatting and rules
- [ ] Blackboard formatting instructions followed (file type, naming, cover information)
- [ ] Blackboard guidance on AI use followed and any required declaration included
- [ ] Uploaded to the Blackboard assignment folder, and the confirmation screen seen, by 1 November
