# 01 Project Plan

## 1. The decision to make first: place and angle

The brief asks for **one place and one angle**, not a portrait of everything. A good angle is a question a real reader would want answered, that an interview can help answer, and that a visit can test.

Decision rule: pick the option where (a) you can visit at least once before 22 October, (b) you can name a specific person to interview this week, and (c) the angle produces a *news or usefulness* hook for at least three different audiences.

### Candidate shortlist (Örebro)

Names and details below came from travel and study-abroad pages and need checking against the official sites before use. Addresses, opening hours and whether programming is in English all change.

| Place | Possible angle (question the project answers) | Interview candidates | Check first |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Wadköping** (open-air heritage district by Svartån, opened as a cultural reserve in 1965) | How does a heritage reserve become a living place for newcomers rather than a museum they visit once? | A craftsperson in one of the workshops, a café owner, a volunteer at a seasonal event | Event calendar for Oct, which workshops are open mid-week, whether staff will talk on record |
| **Kulturkvarteret** (library and culture house) | How does a public library quarter help international students find a foothold in the city in their first term? | A librarian running newcomer or language activities, a student who uses it weekly | Which programmes exist this autumn (language café, study spaces, events), contact for press or communications |
| **Örebro Konsthall / OpenArt** | Can free public art be the easiest first door into a city for someone who does not speak Swedish? | A gallery host or educator, an artist with a current show, a regular visitor | Current exhibition dates, whether OpenArt has any off-season presence in October |
| **A campus space at Örebro University** (library, student union house, a specific study area) | What does a new international student actually need from one campus space in week one, and does it deliver? | A student union officer, a librarian, two first-term students | Permission to photograph or observe, which service is in scope |
| **A market or local initiative** (weekly market, repair café, sports club open session, church or association meal) | How does one recurring local event create belonging for people who arrived with no network? | The organiser, one long-time participant, one newcomer | That it runs on a date you can attend before 22 Oct |

**Chosen place:** Tomasii Restaurang, Karlsdalsallén 63, owned and run by a wife and husband, a family-run restaurant about ten minutes from home that opened on 1 October 2026 in a unit that previously housed another restaurant. See `07_Restaurant_Field_Guide.md`.
**Chosen angle (one sentence):** Exploring the region's claim to be "the place of opportunities" (from the county's tourism place story) through a family's brand-new restaurant, treated as a proposition to investigate (lecturer, 15 Oct). Full plan for the lecturer: `08_Plan_and_Questions_for_Lecturer.md`.
**Why this one and not the others:** It is visitable twice a week without effort, the owner is reachable in person, and the 1 October opening lets the feature catch a family at the very start of something new. Lecturer's guidance in class, 12 October: promote Örebro through a chosen lens, name the selling points and the audience, use statistics and quotations, keep the writer's own voice throughout, send potential questions by Thursday 15 October (grading checks whether they were answered), and defend the choice of place in the plan. Feedback by email on 15 October approved the approach; see `11_Lecturer_Feedback_15Oct.md`.

## 2. Scope decisions

- **One visit minimum, two if possible.** Second visit is for checking details and taking the photograph that all three social media posts will share.
- **One interview minimum (required for the feature).** Aim for two shorter extra conversations (a visitor, a participant) so the feature can show that others experience the place differently, as the brief asks.
- **Four texts:** the feature (the main piece), plus three short posts on Instagram, Facebook and LinkedIn that reuse its core message and one photograph.
- **Theoretical brief:** short, roughly 600 to 900 words, with concepts cited to the editions actually used.
- **Honesty requirement:** the brief says research may reveal "things that could be improved". Keep one critical finding in the feature and in the plan; do not sand it off.

## 5. Risks and fallbacks

- **Interviewee cancels:** have a second name lined up by 15 Oct. A participant or regular visitor counts, per the brief.
- **Place closed or event cancelled:** switch to the second shortlisted place by 17 Oct at the latest; the kit does not change.
- **Cannot visit in person:** the brief says "visit if possible". Document why, rely on the interview plus independent sources, and state the gap in the plan.
