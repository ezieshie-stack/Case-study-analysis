# Text 2: Press release

**Audience:** Local journalists ([[e.g. Nerikes Allehanda, SVT Nyheter Örebro, Sveriges Radio P4 Örebro]])
**Format:** Press release, approx. 350 to 500 words
**Intended platform:** Email to newsdesks, published on [[organisation]]'s newsroom page

> ✎ Only use this format if you have a genuine news angle (something new, timely and local: a new programme, a milestone, an event, a change). If not, swap this text for another format the brief allows and explain in the plan. Structure follows the inverted pyramid: most newsworthy facts first, detail after, boilerplate last. Journalists should be able to cut from the bottom.

**PRESS RELEASE**
**For immediate release** | [[DATE]]

## [[HEADLINE: active verb, the news in under 12 words]]

### [[Subheading: one sentence adding the key detail]]

**ÖREBRO, [[date]].** [[LEAD PARAGRAPH: who, what, when, where, why in two or three sentences. A journalist who reads only this should have the story.]]

> ✎ Smith's news values: significance, localness, balance, timeliness, unusualness, fame. Name which ones this story has in the plan.

[[PARAGRAPH 2: The most important supporting detail: numbers, dates, what is new compared with before. Source every figure.]]

[[PARAGRAPH 3: Quotation from the organiser or a named spokesperson, from your interview, verified in the log. Should add meaning or perspective, not repeat the lead.]]

"[[Exact words.]]" said [[Name, role, organisation]].

[[PARAGRAPH 4: Context and background: what the place is, who it serves, a relevant history fact with a source.]]

[[PARAGRAPH 5: Optional second quotation from a participant or partner, or a practical detail for readers (how to take part, where, when, cost).]]

- Where: [[address]]
- Cost: [[...]]
- More: [[URL]]
