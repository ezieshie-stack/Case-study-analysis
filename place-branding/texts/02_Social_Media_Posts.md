# Texts 2 to 4: One story, one photo, three platforms

> ✎ The lecturer's brief for these three: they are short, they reuse the feature's core message and one photograph (or a few), and the point is to show how you adapt the same material to each platform's audience. Explain the choices in the communication plan. Every figure below is from Internetstiftelsen, *Svenskarna och internet 2025*, table 3.5 and diagram 3.4b (national data, internet users aged 8+). See `10_Orebro_Research_Notes.md` section 6.

> ✎ Take the photo so that it survives three crops: 4:5 portrait for Instagram, and landscape or square for Facebook and LinkedIn. ## Text 3: Facebook

**Audience:** Örebro residents roughly 35 to 65, including families and people living near the restaurant
**Format:** Facebook post: the same photograph plus 120 to 200 words, with a link to the feature
**Intended platform:** Facebook ([[the restaurant's page, or a local Örebro neighbourhood group]])

> ✎ In Sweden Facebook is still where adults follow local life and neighbourhood groups, so this version can carry more text and more practical detail. Write as a neighbour, not as an advertiser. ## Text 4: LinkedIn

**Audience:** Local professionals and potential partners: business networks, food producers, student associations, Örebro University
**Format:** LinkedIn post: the same photograph plus 150 to 250 words
**Intended platform:** LinkedIn ([[your own profile, writing as the student journalist who reported the story]])

> ✎ Persuasive and professional (Smith): open with an insight, prove it with one fact and one quotation, end with one clear ask. Short paragraphs of one or two sentences. Three to five hashtags.

[[HOOK: one line of insight about starting a business in Örebro, drawn from your reporting, not a greeting.]]

[[STORY: the family and the opening in two or three sentences.]]

[[EVIDENCE: one statistic, e.g. restaurants in Sweden had another record year for bankruptcies in 2025 (Visita), and one verified quotation from the owners.]]

[[THE ÖREBRO POINT: what this says about Örebro as "the place of opportunities".]]
