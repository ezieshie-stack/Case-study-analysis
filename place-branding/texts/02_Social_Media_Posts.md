## Text 4: LinkedIn

**Audience:** Örebro's local business community and people considering starting a business
**Purpose:** Share what starting a local business in Örebro has been like in practice
**Format:** LinkedIn post: the same photograph plus 150 to 250 words
**Intended platform:** LinkedIn ([[your own profile, writing as the student journalist who reported the story]])

> ✎ Persuasive and professional (Smith): open with an insight, prove it with one fact and one quotation, end with one clear ask. Short paragraphs of one or two sentences. Three to five hashtags.

[[HOOK: one concrete moment from the owners' story, e.g. when opening became a real possibility. Not a greeting.]]

[[STORY: the family and the opening in two or three sentences.]]

[[EVIDENCE: what helped them start and the biggest unexpected difficulty, with one verified quotation. One context figure if useful, worded with its scope, e.g. "across Sweden, bankruptcies stayed at historically high levels in 2025, with hotels and restaurants among the hardest-hit sectors".]]
