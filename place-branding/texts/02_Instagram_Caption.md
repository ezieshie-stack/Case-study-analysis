# Text 2: Instagram caption

**Audience:** Young Örebro locals, roughly 18 to 30, who choose where to eat from social media
**Format:** Instagram caption, 60 to 120 words, with one photograph from the visit
**Intended platform:** Instagram ([[the restaurant's account, or a local food account]])

> ✎ A different group from the feature's readers: these people already live here, are not looking for a story about settling in, and decide in seconds whether a place is worth a visit. Friedmann: the picture does the showing; the caption adds meaning, voice and the one detail the image cannot carry. Smith: write for the platform, keep the hook in the first line, end with one clear action.
