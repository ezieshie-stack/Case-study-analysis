# Text 4: LinkedIn post

**Audience:** Community organisations and potential partners in Örebro ([[e.g. student associations, integration initiatives, local businesses, Studiefrämjandet, the municipality's culture unit]])
**Format:** LinkedIn post, approx. 150 to 250 words, plus suggested image and hashtags
**Intended platform:** LinkedIn, posted from [[the organisation's page / the organiser's personal profile]]

> ✎ A partner reads for "what is in it for us and our members, and what do you want from us?" Smith's audience analysis: identify the public's interests first, then the benefit, then one clear next step. Tone: professional, direct, first person plural if posted by the organisation. No hard sell, no superlatives you cannot back up. One verified quotation or one verified number gives it credibility.
