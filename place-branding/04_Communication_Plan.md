## 2. What I learned

[[One paragraph on the research: desk sources, the visit(s), the interview(s). Then the two or three findings that shaped the texts, including at least one thing that could be improved.]]

> ✎ Useful frame from Kavaratzis (2004): a city's image is formed through three kinds of communication. Primary communication is what the place does and is (its landscape, infrastructure, organisation and behaviour). Secondary communication is what it deliberately says about itself (advertising, PR, design). Tertiary communication is what others say (word of mouth, media). Say where your findings came from in these terms, for example where the place's own messaging (secondary) matched or diverged from what visitors told you (tertiary) and what you saw (primary). Check the exact terms against the article before citing.
