Hello,

Thank you for agreeing to talk with me. I am a master's student at Örebro University. This interview is for a writing course. I will write a story about your restaurant and about Örebro. It is not an advertisement.

The interview takes about 30 to 40 minutes. You can answer in English or Swedish, whichever is easier. You are both very welcome to take part. You can skip any question, and you can tell me if something should not be printed. I may also ask a few small follow-up questions.

## About you

1. **Please tell me about yourselves. Who are you, and how did this restaurant begin?**

2. **Why did you choose Örebro?**

3. **When did opening a restaurant here start to feel like a real possibility?**

4. **Why this street and this building? Do you know about the restaurant that was here before?**

5. **What do you remember about the opening day, 1 October?**

## Your food

6. **What food do you serve, and what is the story behind it?**

7. **Do you use any ingredients from the Örebro area? Why or why not?**

## Your guests and neighbours

8. **Who comes to eat here? Is it who you expected?**

9. **How have the neighbours welcomed you?**

## Starting the business

10. **What helped you get started? For example people, the municipality, neighbours or other businesses.**

11. **What has been the biggest unexpected difficulty so far?**

12. **Why did you choose to open in the evenings rather than for breakfast or lunch?**

13. **What is it like to run the restaurant as a family?**

14. **Are there local businesses, food producers or groups you would like to work with?**

## Örebro and the future

15. **What should someone who is new in Örebro know before they come here?**

16. **Is Örebro a good place to start something new? Why or why not?**

17. **What would you like to change or improve?**

18. **Where do you see the restaurant one year from now?**

## Practical details

How many seats? Opening hours? How many people work here? Prices? Card or cash? Can guests book a table? Is the menu in English?
